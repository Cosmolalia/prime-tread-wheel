What survives the ratio. Primality and divisibility are properties of the integer, so gold still means prime and each cut color still means divisible by that wheel — at any ratio. The 1:1-only theorems (column law, triangular dead-lines) re-derive from the new base at every ratio, and the probes have mapped how: rational ratios grow their own dead lines; irrational ones grow none — rationality is the exact condition for structure. At a/b a fraction 1/b of laps close clean, and the parity escape runs at exactly 2.00× on its natural classes (m mod 4b) at every ratio — 1:1 is the most legible dial stop, not the most locked. Integer ratios kill the seam by pure algebra. At 2:1 the wheel is a polar Ulam spiral: squares sit one tick inboard of the seam, and Euler's n²−n+41 is the fixed tick k=41 — prime for laps 1–40, dead at 41, zero exceptions. Where to look: dial a wild rational (13:7, 1:11) and watch dead lines crystallize; hit ⇄ for the mirror lattice; then set π and watch all of it vanish.
