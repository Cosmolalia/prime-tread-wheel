# THE UNIQUENESS TEST — is the wheel the only object this simple that does all this?

Date: 2026-10-13. Status: **open challenge spec, flagship item for the next relay
handoff.** Owner of the claim: SylvaN. Formalized by Kimi.

This document converts a philosophy into a discriminating test. Everything
below is labeled [S]/[F]/[G]/[E] per house rules. Predictions are pre-registered
**before** any candidate is surveyed in depth.

## 0. The claim under test [S]

Stated in the user's own causal language, three parts:

1. **Geometry first.** The wheel is a *construction* — a couple of homogenized
   pieces (spoke masks, one phase each, multiplicative composition) — and its
   truths are true *because* the construction is spatially instantiated.
   Arithmetic is downstream (it follows the geometry), not upstream.
2. **Zero overhead.** "We don't need to compute a thing. Just roll." The wheel's
   function is realized by *motion*, not by symbol manipulation. If the spokes
   were physical they would do the whole thing, along with the unit and the
   L-build. There is virtually no overhead: the machine's operation is the
   computation.
3. **Uniqueness at this simplicity.** No *other* object, as simple, carries the
   full bundle (defined below). Test: build one, or score the whole field.

The test exists because part 3 is checkable even though parts 1–2 are
foundational commitments.

## 1. Scoring axes

Every candidate construction is scored A/B/C/C2. Simplicity is compared at
matched capability (a rival doing 10× more with 10× primitives does not win;
the bundle below is the target).

**Axis A — Generative closure [the facts].**
Decides the gate (N prime iff no mask covers N) up to primorial², *and* induces
the constellation structure (quadratic families, the dial, double-shots) as an
internal shadow rather than an external annotation.

**Axis B — Intrinsic verbs [the questions].**
Supports displacement/turning/wiping as *motions of the object itself*, such
that the object's own question-set (wedge distance, dynamic rings, N1) is
well-defined. If an external agent must define "move ring 29," the object does
not own the verb.

**Axis C — Realization economy [SylvaN's pillar, added 2026-10-13].**
The function is realized by a trajectory of the object (rolling), with
actuation cost ∝ laps, not ∝ decisions. No counters, no comparisons, no state
rewrites in the execution path. Parallelism is geometric, not scheduled.

**Axis C2 — Raw-state legibility [the movie-wall frame, added 2026-10-13
midday].** Score: the number of transformations standing between the
object's *raw, unprocessed* post-actuation state and a legible phenomenon
(the constellations, a gap, a count). "Legible" = the phenomenon's units
coincide with the state's units, so no decoding step intervenes. The wheel
after k laps *is* a distribution of holes at integer positions; the coprime-to-6
seats (6k±1 — **[C]** NOT "sexy primes," which are *pairs* differing by 6; the
error was Grok's catch) form a visible band, not a computed output. A sieve's
raw state is a
bitmap that must still be scanned; zeta's raw state is a complex function
whose phenomena (zeros, explicit formula) require contour integration —
forest projections of the same movie: the light returns, but through a
surface with its own grain. Metric: count the decoding transformations
(bitmap→list? list→positions? integral→sum?). **Pre-registered [S]:
Eratosthenes and prime-CAs may tie the wheel on C (rolling is cheap) but
lose C2 — their constellations require a scan/processing pass before anyone
can see them. Designated upset: a prime-generating CA whose spacetime
pattern is self-legible, which would tie C2.**

## 2. Axis C decompressed — what "just roll" actually claims

Mechanism, stated at the present resolution:

- The wheel's state *is* a phase configuration. Its operation is an isometry of
  that configuration (one turn). Settling the gate for every tick in a lap is
  not a procedure executed over ticks — it is a property read out of positions
  after one motion. One actuation processes all ticks simultaneously.
- Contrast class: symbol-rewriting machines (sieve programs, zeta evaluation)
  have execution cost ∝ work. Motion-parallel machines have actuation cost
  ∝ laps and per-tick work cost absorbed into geometry.

**[E] This is not hypothetical.** Physical prime sieves are a documented
lineage: D. N. Lehmer's mechanical sieves and factor tables (c. 1899–1909);
**D. H. Lehmer's bicycle-chain sieve — nineteen loops whose lengths ARE the
moduli, small primes folded to mechanical lengths: 64, 27, 25, 49, 22, 26, 17,
19, 46, 29, 31, 74, 41, 43, 94, 53, 59, 61, 67** (i.e. 2⁶, 3³, 5², 7², 2·11,
2·13, then 17, 19, 2·23, 29, 31, 2·37, 41, 43, 2·47, 53, 59, 61, 67 — each
prime ≤ 67 represented exactly once **[C]** 2026-10-13: the earlier draft
dropped the 2p doubling for 23, 37, 47; GLM's structural recall and the
standard accounts carry 46, 74, 94), with pins marking excluded residues.
**[C]** The same-section merger of this machine with the *photoelectric* gear
sieve is also corrected: those are two machines (bicycle-chain, built 1926,
described 1928 in *The Mechanical Combination of Linear Forms*; photoelectric,
1933, *Amer. Math. Monthly* 40). One chain per ring; the excluded classes were
literally holes in physical things. The number-theory community already built
the physical wheel once. Axis C has historical precedent, not just poetry.

Honest caveats **[G]**:

- **Readout is not free.** Interrogating a tick costs an observation. "Zero
  overhead" means zero *processing* overhead, not zero measurement cost.
- **Physical instantiation at scale is idealized.** Q² ticks exceeds any
  material realization; "physical" means the dynamics admit a mechanical
  model, not that primorial² can be manufactured.
- **Masks are primitives.** The gate's per-prime mask is itself arithmetic
  ("p divides N"). The wheel assumes the masks; whether the unit and the
  L-build can themselves be motion-generated (self-assembling spokes) is an
  open sub-question **[G]**, flagged rather than answered.

## 4. Referee rules

1. **Relabeling doesn't count.** Isomorphic constructions (same primitives,
   same dynamics, renamed) are the same object. Uniqueness is uniqueness up
   to the primitives + composition rules, not up to vocabulary.
2. **Intrinsic only.** A capability scores only if it is internal to the
   object's own dynamics. Bolted-on capability (an external agent defining the
   verbs) fails the axis.
3. **Matched capability.** Simplicity is judged at the bundle, not at the
   candidate's best feature.
4. **Partial results count.** The test's output is a scoreboard, not a verdict
   bit. A rival that takes C and B from the wheel refutes the uniqueness claim
   but sharpens everyone's understanding of where the geometry's ownership
   actually lives.

## 5. Outcomes and what each would mean

- **Refuted:** some candidate matches A+B+C at ≤ simplicity → the wheel is a
  rendering of a broader class; the geometry's claimed priority was an artifact
  of having met the wheel first. That is a real result, filed as such.
- **Confirmed:** wheel sole occupant of the A+B+C corner → the **uniqueness-of-
  joints** result: the wheel is the unique simple object whose *questions* are
  intrinsic and whose *execution* is motion. The geometry's priority becomes a
  theorem-shaped statement rather than a commitment.
- **Partial (predicted):** axis A is shared by everything that touches primes
  (the constellations are common property of CRT-organized machines [E: Ulam
  1963]); axis B+C are where the wheel is alone. If so, the honest slogan is:
  *others carry the facts; the wheel carries the facts plus their adjacency and
  their motions.*

## 6. Why this is the flagship, in light of 2026-10-13's adjudications

This morning's corrections established the frame this test runs in:

- Every anomaly that dissolved (Bateman–Horn families, N1 = Jacobsthal
  windows, the wedge = ω-degradation table) dissolved *into entailments of the
  gate geometry*. The tables are the wheel's shadow, not its master
  (direction-of-explanation correction, 2026-10-13 morning).
- The classical machinery (Legendre symbols, singular series, Jacobsthal's

The test operationalizes: **the wheel is what makes it true, and "it" is the
bundle, not any single fact.**
