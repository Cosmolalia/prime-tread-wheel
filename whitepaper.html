The Prime Tread Wheel — White Paper

Counting numbers on a wheel that grows by one tick per layer, and what the stagger of that growth knows about the primes.

Obi (Sylvan Gaskin), with Claude and Kimi. 2026-10-10.

Standalone summary of the full project: the construction, the classical layer, the stagger field, the exact theorems, the measured statistics, the closed routes, the held claims, the ratio-dial era, the deadness engine, the quadratic rhyme probe, the standing wall, and the method. Everything below is cross-checked against INSIGHTS.md (the lab notebook), PAPER.md (the short paper), the lab/PHASE* artifacts, and the probe suite. 0. Epistemic labels (the contract this paper runs on)

Every claim carries one of four labels:

  • [S] — STATED. A premise or proposed mechanism. Not derived, not proved by plausibility.
  • [F] — FOLLOWS. Actually entailed by preceding statements.
  • [G] — GAP. Resolution stops here; the missing piece is named, not filled with an adjacent theory.
  • [E] — ESTABLISHED. Proved here, or machine-verified by a probe whose exact command and expected output are on record.

Rules that govern the whole document: never increase the resolution of a claim beyond what the mechanism specifies; attack connectors, not endpoints; honest negatives are load-bearing and are kept in writing; superseded readings are marked, not erased; use the smallest sufficient claim.

1. The construction — one rule

[S] Layer m of the wheel holds the m numbers T(m−1)+1 … T(m), where T(m) = m(m+1)/2 is the m-th triangular number. Tick k of layer m sits at angle 2π·k/m. The wheel grows by one tick per layer while the tick never changes size. Laid flat, the layers are the rows of Floyd's triangle; rolled onto polar coordinates, every rational angle in (0,1] owns exactly one spoke, and irrationals own none.

From this one rule, with nothing added:

  • [F] Ratio as slip. Adjacent layers misalign by the fractional part of the running count. A spoke a/b re-opens every b layers, exactly when b divides the layer index. At the seam the slip is zero and layers stack in straight columns; at the half-turn the slip is one half and the stack forms bricks.
  • [F] The multiplexer reading. Placing n on layer m computes n mod m spatially: each lap is a complete residue system, and the frame length equals the lap index, so frame growth is the enumeration of moduli. No division is performed anywhere; the slot spacing is the operation. (Caveat recorded in PAPER.md §8: on even laps the origin sits a half-turn off, since T(m−1) ≡ m/2 mod m; "slot" means steps since the lap's own origin.)

2. The classical layer — what was already known, made visible

  • [E] Channel capacity is two. Tick k of layer m lies on spoke a/b with g = gcd(k,m), a = k/g, b = m/g, and its number is x = g(gb² − b + 2a)/2. For g ≥ 3 the visit number divides its occupant (g odd → g|x; g even → (g/2)|x with cofactor > 1). Every spoke carries at most two primes: its first visit, and its second visit when b is odd. Proven; verified for every visit to layer 5000. The divisibility is noted in Math::PlanePath's GcdRationals documentation (after Fortnow 2004); the two-prime and φ(m) corollaries we have not found stated.
  • [F] Seats. A prime can sit only on a coprime tick (gcd(k, m) = 1) — one line from the B2 eligibility law — so the generic lap offers exactly φ(m) candidate ticks (count verified to layer 800). Parity caveat (B5/B6): on laps m ≡ 2 (mod 4), T(m−1) is odd and every coprime k is odd, so every seat value is even — no prime ever sits on a seat there; those laps' primes all sit on the even gcd = 2 ticks at 2.00× density. "φ(m) seats" is the generic-lap count; the parity-escape laps re-allocate them.
  • [E] The parity axis. T(m−1) and ⌊m/2⌋ share parity (triangular numbers run odd, odd, even, even), so evenness is pinned to the half-turn axis. The half-turn spoke holds 2, 8, 18, 32, 50. Checked to layer 5000.
  • [E, classical] The layer balance. Unit arrows at the fresh ticks sum to μ(m) — the fresh ticks are the primitive m-th roots of unity — and the running total is the Mertens function M(n), tied to RH by M(n) = O(n^(1/2+ε)).
  • [E, known] Junction lanes. The opening tick T(m−1)+1 is the lazy caterer number; 8(T(m−1)+1) = (2m−1)² + 7, so an odd prime p divides a lane number only when −7 is a square mod p. Through layer 100,000 the lane holds 9,863 primes vs the Bateman–Horn prediction 9,836 (OEIS A055469).
  • [E, checked] The wall (PAPER.md §7). A knockout test hides each layer's primes and asks which smaller wheels recover them: about half the wheels below the square root, the same share as for any interval of that length. The wheel shows the sieve's boundary clearly. It does not move it.

3. The reframe — the stagger is the primary object

Sylvan's framing, now the project's: the wheel's primary object is not the primes. It is the stagger of the number line as the wheel grows relative to all prior layers. Primes fall out of reading the stagger.

  • [S] The stagger field. For prime p, its shadow on lap m lands at tick k ≡ o_p(m) = −T(m−1) (mod p). The stagger field at step m is the tuple {o_p(m)} over all prior primes.
  • [F] The whole number line's stagger at step m is the single counter m, read in every prime modulus at once. Each prime reads a different base-p digit of the one counter. The field's lap-to-lap shift is o_p(m+1) − o_p(m) = −m (mod p).
  • [F] Parabolic confinement. Each o_p(m) = −T(m−1) (mod p) is periodic in m with exact period p (period 4 for p = 2), and for odd p visits exactly (p+1)/2 of p phases: T(m−1) = ((2m−1)² − 1)/8 is a quadratic image, which takes (p+1)/2 values mod p. Each arc sweeps a discrete palindromic parabola across laps and is forever confined away from (p−1)/2 of its phases. Proven in two lines; the former "zero violations, p < 200" is now a certificate, not the evidence.
  • [F] Exact aperiodicity. The joint field over the first r primes recurs with period lcm(2…p_r) ≈ e^{p_r}: the first 25 primes give ~10³⁶ laps. Aperiodicity is proved, not observed.
  • [E] Stagger-determinism (the platonic map). Computing the arc cover from {o_p(m)} alone — no values, no arithmetic on values — reproduces the actual first-prime offset of every lap m = 4…5000 (4,997 laps, 0 mismatches; re-validated 400/400 at m ≤ 2·10⁴ and 4,997/4,997 against the shadow map with the exact witness rule). The first-prime function is a pure function of the stagger configuration. The wheel contains the complete answer to prime placement in uncompressed form.
  • [F] The movie, not the frame. Within a lap, shadows arrange freely (no k-direction constraint; covers can pile up). Across laps, orbits are confined parabolas. Any wheel-native proof must be a law on stagger orbits, not a per-lap covering constraint.
  • [E] The exact composite-ness rule. Tick k on lap m is shut iff ∃ prime p ≤ √(T(m−1)+k) with k ≡ −T(m−1) (mod p). Arcs from p > √v never witness compositeness. The √T(m) sieve limit is the exact witness bound. With the per-lap limit restored, the stagger model IS exact primality (63/63 laps, model W == nextprime(T(m−1)) − T(m−1)). Two sessions of probes got this limit right; the one probe that dropped it watched the model eat the primes it was supposed to locate. The limit is load-bearing, demonstrated from both sides.
  • [E] Zero internal field correlation (the CRT lock, N1). The joint orbit over a finite prime set is the full Cartesian product of the individual triangular images with multiplicity, by the Chinese remainder theorem — verified exactly on (5,7), (3,5,7), (5,11). The stagger field has no internal correlations to exploit. Its entire law is the single quadratic counter s(m) = −T(m−1). The wall is located to one point: one quadratic walk interacting with the compositeness of the values it walks over.

4. The exact-theorem cluster — the wheel's own certificates

  • [F] B1 — the visible set is exactly the primes. Tick k at layer m is covered iff N = T(m−1)+k is divisible by some prime p ≤ m. Any composite N ≤ T(m) has a factor ≤ √N ≤ √(T(m)) < m. Therefore uncovered ⟹ N is m-rough ⟹ N is prime. The wheel at layer m is the √N sieve rendered as occlusion; each layer certifies primality of numbers up to ~m²/2 using witnesses ≤ m, with no value-side act needed within the lap. Audited on laps 2…2000 (149,001 uncovered ticks): zero non-primes uncovered, zero primes covered except the trivial self-cover of p ≤ m. Sylvan: "it IS a physical geometric sieve" — confirmed.
  • [F] B2 — the column law. A prime N = T(m−1)+k can occur only if gcd(k,m) = 1, or gcd = 2 with m ≡ 2 (mod 4) (the parity escape). Audited m = 2…3000: 100% of 316,048 primes in eligible columns, zero violations. Eligibility is a free gcd certificate, forever. Every rational pole is composite at all depths beyond its innermost point with no occlusion needed; "the 2-prime rows bottom" is exact algebra.
  • [F] B3 — the triangular-offset theorem. A dot recurs every layer with a closed-form factorization iff its offset is triangular: N = T(m−1) + m − c factors as (m−j)(m+j+1)/2 exactly when c = T(j). Audited m = 20…1500: all five triangular offsets are pure-dead with zero exceptions; every non-triangular offset tested carries primes. The predictable positions are the dead ones, and their addresses are the triangular numbers.
  • [F] B4 — forced-composite endpoints. T(m) is composite for all m ≥ 3 and T(m)−1 for all m ≥ 4: the lap's last two ticks are arithmetically forbidden primes. Identities, not geometry.
  • [E] B5 — the m ≡ 2 (mod 4) theorem. Seat-primes are identically zero on these laps; all primes sit on non-seat (non-coprime) ticks. The seat test is misaligned from value-divisibility by exactly m/2.
  • [E] B6 — the parity escape. The eligible even-k ticks on m ≡ 2 (mod 4) laps carry exactly 2.00× the prime density of coprime columns (0.1734 vs 0.0866, laps 3…3000). Mechanism: pure parity filter.
  • [F] B7 — the mirror lift. T(m−1) + T(m) = m², so the tick involution k ↔ m − k pairs values summing to exactly m²: the seat palindrome lifts to values as v ↔ m² − v. Eligibility is preserved exactly (gcd(k, m) = gcd(m − k, m), including the gcd = 2 parity escape), and the map is a bijection on ticks. Two measured closures fall out of the one identity. For odd m no mirror prime pair can exist — m² is odd, so one of v, m² − v is an even number ≥ 4 — and on even m a mirror pair is exactly a Goldbach representation of m² with both summands in the layer window: a rare coincidence, which is §6.3's mirror-compensation null in one line, while the exact bijection is why the seat palindrome stays perfect on the pattern side precisely where value-side compensation is dead. (Verified: zero odd-m pairs over m < 3000; 11,085 even-m pairs, every one summing to m².) The mirror probe was the wrong instrument for a per-lap law and the right one for the Goldbach window.
  • [E] C4 (relocation anti-correlation). Followed every prime to the next layer's same angular column: 0.1413 vs 0.2289 random expectation. The prime does NOT follow its column. What is predictable is where the shadows will be; the primes are the negative space of a predictable moiré. Mechanism open, descriptive only.

5. The measured statistics — what the field does at scale

  • [E] The width–strikers invariant. Mouth width W (the initial run of shut ticks at a lap's left edge) tracks the number of distinct outside primes jointly holding it: spearman(W, mouth_distinct) = 0.995 at m ~ 10⁴, 0.981–0.992 at m ~ 2.6·10⁸. Scale-stable across eight orders of magnitude. The distribution shape is not scale-invariant; this identity is.
  • [E] All aggregate value-side statistics tested are iid-indistinguishable. Quad-counter vs iid-lap nulls: mouth marginals (KS gap 0.060), lagged fingerprints (P10, P11 — the pre-registered ring-2 parity hint failed replication and is closed). The law that drives everything is invisible in every aggregate W-statistic. [F] Reason (P12): with the sieve limit restored, the stagger model is exact primality, so every aggregate value-side statistic of the real wheel is ordinary prime-gap statistics. The wheel's lawful geometry lives on the pattern side only.
  • [E] The clash factor (Obi's bet). Layer counts sit a fixed ~11% below the independent-rings guess ∏(1−1/p), converging down to e^γ/2 = 0.89054 (0.89092 at 1e5, 0.89064 at 1e6, 0.89060 at 1.8M layers; numbers to 1.62e12). Mechanism: rings p, q clash every pq ticks; the independent guess books phantom clashes at distances longer than the number line, and dropping them is the haircut. The classical identification: true survivor density is 1/ln N ≈ 1/(2 ln m) (N ≈ m²/2), while the Mertens product over p ≤ m is e^(−γ)/ln m — their ratio is exactly e^γ/2. The clash mechanism is the wheel-native account of Mertens' third theorem: the identity is [E], the phantom-clash reading [S]. Pre-registered replication (PHASE7): nine frozen predictions scored 8/9 on a cross-machine run; the one miss (sparsest-layer count 36,720 vs 35,300±700) is upward — layer counts are more uniform than an iid-style tail model allows. Full scorecard: lab/PHASE7_prereg_scorecard.md.
  • [E] U(m)/m tracks Mertens. The realized arcs thin each lap ~4% more than independent sieve (median ratio 0.961 against e^-γ/ln m), with large per-lap fluctuations. The quadratic alignment is sieve-equivalent in aggregate, lap-specific in detail.
  • [E] The record layer. Layer m = 264,800,157: first prime 766 ticks above the floor (ifirst_off = 767), trailing shut-run 15 — the record's right edge is nearly shut too. Anatomy (field notes, the primary record): 109 outside strikers — arcs from primes above the seat threshold — redundantly hold the mouth shut (1.68× cover; 270 of its 497 mouth seats carry exactly one arc). 57 of the 766 shut ticks are holes: no striker arc lands on them, yet every one is composite with both factors > 766 — invisible to every prime ≤ 766, covered by inside primes in (766, m]. No B1 tension: B1's witness set is p ≤ m, and these ticks have their witnesses; the measured invariant is the striker count, not a prime range (width–strikers, above). The record mouth is a generic-sieve pile-up, fully explained by striker count.

6. Honest negatives — closed routes, load-bearing, do not reopen blind

  1. [E] Orbit correlations: none exist (CRT, exact factorization, N1).
  2. [E] Hole co-factor structure: nothing wheel-native — holes are exactly W-rough numbers; the 74% semiprime fraction is the Mertens rough-density prediction, matched per-W-bucket.
  3. [E] Mirror compensation: dead at both scales (r = −0.07 at 10⁴, +0.02 at 2.6·10⁸). The seat palindrome does not propagate to value-side mouth geometry.
  4. [E] The per-arc thinning law: refuted by test (N8) — the parabolic image selects which phases an arc visits, but its landing distribution over [1,p] is ~uniform.
  5. [E] Lagged fingerprints of the counter: null (P10; P11 → record-repair saga, §15.4 — two frozen texts disagreed on the predicted sign; the honest phase-stratified null leaves a 2.2σ residual carrying no weight pending pre-registered replication).
  6. [E] C4 anti-follow (0.62×): mechanism open, descriptive only.
  7. [E] Strip enrichment: 1.09× lean, real but small — the strips' visual dominance is candidate density, not prime concentration. Strip hearts (the exact poles) are 100% dead.
  8. [E] Census axis: closed by MATCH — certified bands match Hardy–Littlewood/Cramér (−0.25σ, −1.16σ; max gap 274 vs the 540 record below 10¹²). The wheel natively counts a one-witness hardness class (N = pᵃ·q, p ≤ bound < q) that classical π₂ undercounts by ~92×; the exact Lehmer enumeration reproduces a dumped band's count to 0.000σ.
  9. [E] ω-map spatial structure: NULL vs honest nulls (the naive iid null is decisively rejected — small-prime determinism lives in the base, not the null).
  10. [E] Retained ring-phase structure: NULL across 16 layers vs alignment-matched nulls. A lag-0 excursion was root-caused as sieve-lattice self-alignment (fixed layer spacing shares small factors) — standing lesson: periodically-spaced samples need alignment-matched nulls.

Consequence: "a massive wheel reveals new statistics" is dead by measurement. All three statistics axes are measured-closed.

7. The held claims

  • [S] C1 — the halves-claim. From m = 21 up, every lap has ≥ 1 interval prime strictly each side of the midpoint. Verified with zero violations over 999,980 laps (m = 21…1,000,000, N to ~5×10¹¹, MR cross-validated); plus the record layer and height families. Margins: med 15/16, max 300/299, lap-level correlation 0.011. Worst-margin trend: log(max W) vs log(ln N) has slope 2.19 ± 0.08 — the claim gets safer with scale (beyond 10⁴ laps the nearest prime stays ≥ 0.99·H from the midpoint). [F] The proof door closes by reduction (PHASE4b): B1 says the wheel IS the √N sieve, so C1 ⟺ "∃ prime in [T(m−1)+1, T(m−1)+⌊m/2⌋]" — an interval of length ≈ 0.7√N at height N ≈ m²/2, about 3× shorter than Legendre's conjecture at comparable height. A wheel-native proof is impossible in principle; "zero structural support" is now explained, not just observed (free rings can cover either half of every layer 21–60; the width bound never obstructs a half-cover anywhere in the A048670 range). Status: [S, Legendre-hard by reduction, empirically bulletproof to 1M laps]. Paper value migrates from the claim to the instrument (the wedge, §10).
  • [S] C2 — no-empty-layer (the parent conjecture, OEIS A066888: every lap holds a prime). Directly verified to layer 10⁹ (3.13 billion Miller–Rabin tests, exact below 3.3·10²⁴), extended through exhaustive prime-gap data to layer 2,828,427,124. Structure-supported to layer 57 (confined rings; free rings wipe every layer 41–391, so structure alone proves a prime only to 57), walk-protected beyond. Baker–Harman–Pintz guarantees primes only in windows of length N^0.525 around N — wider than the layer width √(2N) for large N, so the promised prime can sit past the layer it was invoked to save: BHP settles no layer, even non-effectively, and the RH-class O(√N log N) bound fails the same width comparison. The verified residue is finite (layers ≤ 2,828,427,124 by the gap tables); the open residue is infinite. Cramér-grade gap heuristics imply the claim trivially — which is why the trend is visible and the proof isn't. Worst known bottom margin: 766 ticks at layer 264,800,157.

8. The ratio-dial era — the variable tread

The canonical wheel scales 1:1 (lap m brings m ticks). The variable tread (variable.html) sets lap length to L(m) = max(1, round(m·r)) for any ratio r — numbers keep their order, only the lattice reflows. What survives, what doesn't, and what new laws appear:

  • [E] L1 — no all-prime line exists anywhere in ratio space. 165 ratios × ~1400 families ≈ 230k family scans: zero families with ≥ 8 samples 100% prime. At rational r the obstruction is exact (N(m) is a quadratic quasi-polynomial; a nonconstant such sequence takes composite values).
  • [E] L2 — rational ⟺ has dead lines. Pure-dead families at every rational a/b tested (always including rim families), at zero irrationals. r = 1's triangular offsets are one instance; every rational has its own. The exact indexing formula for dead offsets at general a/b was the D8 question, closed in §9.
  • [E] L3 — the parity escape is universal at exactly 2.00×. At every ratio, the (m mod 4 × k-parity) classes where N is forced odd carry exactly 2.00× prime density; forced-even classes are prime-deserts. An apparent "diluted escape" at non-1:1 ratios was an aliasing artifact: re-scanning at resolution m mod 4b shows every rational has the same pure lock as 1:1 — deadness of an odd-k tick is decided purely by B[m] mod 2, periodic with period dividing ~4b. The 1:1 wheel is not special; it is just legible.
  • [F+E] L4 — seam laws. Integer r: base(m) = r·T(m−1) exactly, seam composite ∀m ≥ 3. Non-exceptional rationals: seam prime-normal ~7%.
  • [F] D5 — the clean-lap law. A lap closes clean (starts at the seam or a half-turn from it) with fraction exactly 1/b at reduced a/b; the lap-start leftover hops among finitely many phases — the partial lock is periodic, never chaotic.
  • [E] D7 — r = 2 is the Ulam spiral, verified exactly. Squares sit at frac 1/2; Euler's n²−n+41 is the fixed tick k = 41 and breaks at m = 41 exactly as the theorem says; Ulam's prime-rich diagonals are constant seam offsets k = 2m + c converging on the square ray.
  • [E] The census — 9,216 integer-ratio offset forms + 176 seam classes. Dead ⟺ square discriminant, past the unit boundary (0/9,216 square-D forms contain a prime; the 41/176 square-D seam classes showing 1–2 primes are unit-factor leaks at t = 0…1 only). Euler is champion but not unique: N = 3m²−3m+23 (D = −267, h = 2) has streak 19 and density 0.430; the line exists only from m = 4 (the lap length must reach its constant 23), so the streak runs from line onset, not from m = 1. Heegner reachability is ratio-gated: twelve of the thirteen known class-number-one orders occur somewhere in range (nine fundamental Heegner discriminants plus four non-fundamental orders make thirteen; "twelve" is the census's reach, not a canonical count), but the champions −43, −67, −163 occur at fixed offsets ONLY at a = 2 — 2:1 is the complete Heegner host. Where the wheel's algebra is strongest (the seam), survival is marginal.
  • [E] G5 closed — the irrational substrate is Sturmian. At r = √2, φ, π, e the lap word has factor complexity p(n) = n+1 through n = 12; rational controls saturate as theory demands. The sieve still holds at irrational ratios (zero gold ticks violate the forced residue exclusions), and beyond the sieve: nothing detectable (χ² ordinary, lag-1 autocorrelation ≤ 2.3σ, sign-inconsistent). Ordered floor, sieve furniture, no deed to the land.
  • [E] The GPU frontier. The roll engine (one ring per GPU lane, closed-form jump seeding, stamp kernel with reciprocal-estimate first-stamp indexing) runs ~33M certified numbers/s at layer 6·10⁹, ~100M/s at 282M; verified exactly against CPU span sieves and deterministic MR (6,002/6,002 primes, 1,952/1,952 composites at a layer-6e9 dump-band, N ~ 1.8·10¹⁹). Bottleneck story, three times corrected: bandwidth → issue-rate → critical-path serialization — one thread per ring, and the p = 2 thread walks the full window in W/2 sequential iterations. A window-parallel kernel prototype (Phase 0.6) is bit-exact vs production and recovers ~95.6% of kernel time (18.2 → 0.77 ms/layer under live-window co-tenancy, 24×); integration into the production engine is queued as a user decision.
  • [F, certified exact] The witness layer. stamp_kernel atomicAdds a per-tick ω_small(N) alongside the bool: every certified tick carries its small-prime witness count. Semantics certified deterministically: the exact Lehmer enumeration of the one-witness class N = pᵃ·q (p ≤ bound < q) or prime power reproduces a dumped band's count exactly (165,861 = 165,861, 0.000σ). The first audit caught a duplicated prime 2 in every prime-table cache — counts exposed instantly what the boolean engine was blind to.

9. The deadness engine — one engine behind every dead line

Three layers, in order of consultation:

  1. Content (Layer 1). Within a constant-L block the seam values are an arithmetic progression with difference L; if gcd(first, L) > 1 the whole block is composite. Universal, exact, sieve-free.
  2. Quasi-polynomial factorization (Layer 2). On each residue class m = R·t + r (R = 2b·2^ν), the seam N(t) is an exact quadratic in t. If the discriminant is a perfect square, N factors over ℚ and the class is identically composite. Exhibit, 3:8, m = 16t+2: N = 48t² + 16t + 1 = (4t+1)(12t+1). 3:8 has all 16 classes factorable — fully dead by algebra alone. Euler's n²−n+41 is a NON-factorable quadratic (D = −163) living on a live class at 2:1.
  3. The sieve (Layer 3) — only after layers 1–2 pass does arithmetic get consulted.

[F→E] D8 — the closed form (PHASE5). Writing L[m] = (a·m + c(m))/b with c(j) periodic mod b, the r²-terms of B² and 4AC in Δ(r) = B² − 4AC cancel identically for every coprime (a,b): the seam discriminant is a quasi-polynomial of polynomial degree ≤ 1, period b (pairing law proved: P = 0 iff b odd, P = b/2 iff b even; 0 violations over coprime b ≤ 12, a ≤ 15). Consequence: r ranges over a residue system mod b, so y² = Δ(r) is a finite union of b exact square tests — no Pell, no elliptic curve; the expected genus obstruction never materializes. Dead classes = {r mod b : Δ(r) square} ∪ {content-killed classes}, computable in O(b) exact arithmetic with no sieve. All landmarks pass (3:8 fully dead; 5:8 half/half; 7:4 content-dead; b = 4 ⟺ a ≡ ±1 mod 8), and the formula predicted and confirmed 9 dead pairs not on the published seam map (3:2, 5:2, 7:2, 8:3, 8:5, 9:2, 9:5, 10:3, 11:2) — the map is extended. Residual openness, stated honestly: (a,b)-parameterized compression of the square tests (the sum is Dedekind-sum-like; no simpler form known) and Layer-1 one-sidedness (content-1 ≠ live; the sieve owns that territory).

Unification: this one engine sits behind L2's dead lines, the seam map, and the Ulam connection. The dead/live boundary everywhere is: square discriminant (factors identically) vs not (can be prime).

10. The quadratic rhyme probe (PHASE6, 2026-10-10) — checked and closed

A March 2026 study (three independent Claude instances) had found a spectral universality across self-similar graphs: the Menger sponge's Laplacian carries the factor x² − (d+2)x + 2 with multiplicity locked to dimension d, irrational spectrum since (d+2)² − 8 is not a square. Both sides of the lab had square-discriminant classifiers — the wheel's deadness engine and the fractal's spectral lock — and the question was whether the rhyme is a corridor.

  • [E] The universality, extended into new territory. Re-derived all three doc cases exactly (characteristic-polynomial factorization over ℚ). Menger d = 4 (new): predicted factor x² − 6x + 2, roots 3 ± √7, multiplicity exactly 4 — the conjecture now has d = 2, 3, 4. Carpet level 2 (new): the factor persists at multiplicity 2. Vicsek d = 3 (new): all-integer spectrum (the K₁,₈ star). Proof upgrade: D(d) = (d+2)² − 8 is a perfect square only at d = 1 (factor-pair argument, two lines; exhaustively checked d ≤ 2000) — irrationality of the spectral roots is proved universal for remove-center d ≥ 2. It remains a conjecture that the factor appears at multiplicity d in general.
  • [E] Surprise rule, against the doc itself: the doc's "spectrum symmetric about S/2" claim is false as a multiset statement at Menger level 1 (mult(2) = 5 vs mult(3) = 1, exact). Carpet L1 is exactly symmetric; sponge L1 only partially. Logged as a doc correction, alongside two smaller ones (the P²·S = cell-count encoding works only at d = 3; the universality is contact-convention-dependent — Vicsek pieces touch at points, and the junction graph is the right object, not face contact).
  • [F→E, closed] The corridor verdict: wallpaper, closed by sign. All 271 wheel seam quadratics sampled have non-negative coefficients (C = 0 in 16 classes, never negative) — a counter polynomial with non-negative coefficients has no strictly positive root. The fractal spectral factor has both roots strictly positive. The two polynomial families are provably disjoint: no wheel class polynomial can be the fractal factor. The shared classifier — perfect-square discriminant of an integer quadratic ⟺ ℚ- factorability — is the quadratic formula, genus 0, ancient. Analogy, not identity. Labeled as such.
  • [E] The result that survives on its own — the dead/live boundary is sawtooth-carried. Strip the sawtooth (the periodic rounding corrections c(j), a Dedekind-sum-like object) out of Δ(r) and keep only the ratio-side ramp: it agrees with the true dead/live classification on 135/271 classes — 49.8%, a coin flip. The sawtooth flips the other half; 47 of 57 ratios change their dead-class set entirely. The all-dead a = 1 row is invisible to ratio geometry (the ramp predicts almost nothing dead; truth says everything dead). The ratio sets the scale; the wheels write the law. And the sawtooth is the same object PHASE5 pinned as D8's true obstruction — one object, two roles.

11. The standing wall, precisely located

[G] The routing wall. The stagger field determines everything (stagger-determinism), but the only known reading is the uncompressed O(m) simulation. No compressed wheel-native law of the field is known. The within-lap instance is closed by B1 (the wheel IS the sieve); the mouth/window instance at W ≪ m stands. The wall's true name, from the roll-engine analysis: holding every prime up to √N is the information price of an exact certificate — any deterministic exact sieve must pay it. The doors past it are different mechanisms, not optimizations: probabilistic certificates (Miller–Rabin) and structure (decoding the stagger field).

[G→ measurable] The wedge (F2). Free rings wipe every layer 41–391; the realized turning never does. The realized turning is not a random point in turning-space — it is one quadratic curve (the stagger law, proved). So the parent conjecture reads: the quadratic curve never enters the wipe set. The wipe set is characterized; the curve is characterized; the per-lap distance between them has never been measured. That distance is the conjecture's true object. One-flip wipe distance: every realized turning in scope is ≥ 2 moves from every wipe turning (consistency, entailed by the verified conjecture to layer 2.8B). The true Hamming-distance instrument (J1c) needs SAT/anneal machinery — jointly queued with the second lab session.

12. The method — what this project is besides its theorems

The wheel project runs mechanism-first theoretical science as an operating procedure, and the procedure itself is a deliverable:

  1. Epistemic labels on every claim ([S]/[F]/[G]/[E]), with the rule that [F] must actually follow — a gap left honestly open beats an elegant theorem pasted over the wrong mechanism.
  2. Predictions pre-registered before checks, with binding stop conditions (the AUTONOMY chain of 2026-10-10 ran seven phases on this rule; its most spectacular deviation, +3,869σ, was root-caused as a prediction-specification error, not a discovery — huge σ-deviations are more often spec errors than physics).
  3. Honest negatives as load-bearing closures. Ten closed routes (§6) are kept in writing with their refutations; the territory map (INSIGHTS.md 2026-10-09 dusk) organizes every standing claim so closed routes cannot be reopened blind.
  4. Instrument before theorem. The wedge distance, the witness layer, the ratio dial, the GPU frontier: where a claim cannot be proved, the project builds the instrument that would measure it.
  5. Corrections published in place. The bandwidth-wall claim, the thinning law, the "semiprime channel," the 2.000 parity lift, the fractal symmetry claim — five superseded readings, each retracted in the same document that carried it, each retraction load-bearing.

Two falsified priors produced standing method lessons: huge σ-deviations are more often specification errors than discoveries; and when a "hard problem" smells like Diophantine genus, first check whether the variable is free or periodic — periodicity trivializes curves.

15.5 PHASE 15 — the displacement curve and its two loads

Object A (25 fixed rings ≤ 97, all 244 finite layers): the displacement d grows ~m/13.5, and the constant is not free. Two-load decomposition, exact predictors: d ≈ 0.215·U + 0.051·m (fit R² = 0.974), where U(m) counts home-uncoverable rough ticks (density → e^(−γ)/ln 97 ≈ 0.123). Displaced rings harvest rough ticks (~⅓ of the slope, several per ring via mod-p collisions) and re-cover composite ticks whose home coverage they broke (~⅔). The 1/13.5 arithmetic: 0.215·(e^(−γ)/ln 97) + 0.051 ≈ 0.077 ≈ 1/13.0 against measured 1/13.4. [checked; mechanism reading S]

Two consequences, both confirmed in sign. (a) The slope is ring-budget-contingent. At B = 53 the finite points sit a consistent +2.2 above the B = 97 prediction (fewer rings, less collision freedom); the B = 31 wheel cannot cover 60 ticks at any displacement — a new Jacobsthal bracket, j(31#) ≤ 60. Constants calibrated at one B do not transfer. (b) Under the native schedule the linear law breaks. With rings = primes ≤ √T(m): d/P ≈ 0.49–0.59 stable across a 2.3× m-range (harvest efficiency rises with ring density — d falls below the fixed-B floor), d/m declines monotonically 0.0570 → 0.0533 → 0.0475 → 0.0433 across m = 263…600, and the m = 1000 sparse MILP (2 MB matrix, the dense build's 19.8 GB OOM is dead engineering) returned d = 38 incumbent against the law's prediction 36.2 — 5% above, inside the historical scatter. Sublinear: d ≈ 0.5·m/(2 ln m), i.e. d/m ~ 1/(4 ln m).
